Our Story

A Love Worth Celebrating

I & J

It started with a borrowed umbrella in a rainstorm outside a bookshop in Florence — the kind of coincidence that feels inevitable in retrospect. James had been there for a conference; Isabella had lived nearby for years. Neither expected anything other than a dry walk home.

"Some people arrive in your life and rearrange all the furniture."

Three years of laughter, travel, long dinners, and quiet mornings later, James got down on one knee at that very same bookshop — on a rainy Thursday afternoon, just to be safe.

Now, surrounded by the people we love most, we begin the next chapter. We cannot wait to celebrate with you.
